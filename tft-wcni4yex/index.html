                How to play

                • Goal: be the last of eight teams standing. Lose a fight and you lose HP; at 0 you are out.
                • Build: buy players in the Shop with gold, then tap a player and a hex to put her on your court. Your level sets how many can play.
                • Upgrade: three copies of a player merge into a 2-star; three 2-stars make a 3-star.
                • Traits: players share game roles, club levels and archetypes. Field enough of one and its bonus switches on (see Traits). Pin and Utility players are wild cards.
                • Fights play themselves, rally style. Front-row Liberos and Middles take the hits; hitters score.
                • Gold: income every round, more on a win or loss streak. Reroll the shop for 1, buy XP for 4.
                • Extras: pick an augment at 2-1, 3-2 and 4-2; take a free player from the carousel at the start of stages 3 to 7.
                • Look closer: hold a player (or press I) for her card; tap a team in Players to scout their board.
                • Keys: R reroll, X buy XP, S sell, N next round, Escape skips a fight, arrow keys move around the court.

                Carousel

                Lowest HP picks first. Take one player for free; if time runs out you get the first open one.

                Pick an augment

                It lasts the whole game. If time runs out you get the first one.
